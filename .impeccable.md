# Design context — T&L Nails Tampa

Read this before changing anything visual on the site (`site/`) or the intake tool (`intake/`). The source of truth is the Claude Design handoff (`project/TL Nails Site.dc.html`). If this file and the design disagree, the design wins, and this file should be updated.

## Brand and audience

- **Business:** T&L Nails, a nail salon in Tampa since 2003, with two locations: Kennedy Blvd and Davis Islands. Services cover manicures, pedicures, enhancements (acrylic, builder gel, Gel-X), nail art, lashes and waxing.
- **Audience:** local clients booking on their phones, often between other errands. They want price, time, location and a fast way to book.
- **Personality:** calm, polished, warm. It's a neighborhood salon that feels like a treat, not a spa brochure. Luxury comes from restraint and spacing, not ornament.
- **Not:** clinical, loud, trendy-neon, or glossy-corporate. No stock "beauty" clichés like gold glitter, script fonts or emoji.

## Visual system

### Color

The tokens live in `site/css/site.css` under `:root`. Use them, and never introduce a new hex value.

- **Grounds:** `--bg` #FBF4F1 is the warm blush page. `--soft` and `--tint` are for quiet panels.
- **Ink:** `--ink` #3A2A2E for headings. The text ramp runs `--text-lede` › `--text-body` › `--text-muted` › `--text-meta` › `--text-hint`.
- **Accents:**
  - `--mauve` #A76A78 is the primary accent: links, eyebrows, the main button.
  - `--deep` #5E3A44 is plum, used for dark bands and secondary buttons.
  - `--rose` and `--blush` are supporting tints.
- **Gold:** `--gold` #BE9A46 only for small marks such as stars and badges, never for large fills.
- **Gradients:** `--grad-hero`, `--grad-plum`, `--grad-feature` and `--grad-foot` are the only gradients.
- **Rules:** use hairlines (`--rule`), not boxes.

The site is light-only. The intake tool mirrors these tokens and adds a dark theme.

### Type

- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond, weight 600. Use it for headings, prices on the service page, and the wordmark (uppercase with .16em tracking until there's a logo). `.h2` is 54px desktop with `line-height: normal`. Keep that: it's the whitespace the design relies on.
- **Body and UI:** Manrope, 400–600.
  - Ledes are 17px/1.7.
  - Body copy is 13–15px/1.6–1.75.
- **Labels and eyebrows:** Manrope 600 at 10–12px, uppercase, with wide tracking (.14–.3em) in `--mauve`.
- No other typefaces.

### Shape and texture

- **Zero border radius everywhere:** buttons, inputs, cards, images and chips.
- **Petal trail:** a full-height SVG texture (`assets/petals/`) running down the page. Sections marked `[data-petals]` repeat it, and `js/site.js` aligns them so the trail reads as one continuous line. Don't break that alignment, and don't add other textures.
- **Shadow:** only on the primary mauve button (a soft, tinted drop shadow). Nothing else.
- **Striped placeholders:** these mark missing photography. They're intentional until real photos arrive.

### Layout

- **1440px content frame:** `--frame`, centered, with `--gutter` at 56px. Everything sits inside it at every width, photos included. Only backgrounds run full-bleed.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - The desktop layout (the 1440 comps) applies from 1024px up.
  - Below 1024 the site uses the mobile layout (the 390 comps).
  - From 768 to 1023, the mobile layout gets the 56px gutter and two-column grids.
- **Hero:** `clamp(640px, 55vw, 790px)` tall.
- **Header:** 86px.
- **Mobile sticky bar:** Book and Menu buttons. It's hidden over the hero and slides up after it.
- **Tap targets:** 44px minimum.

### Motion

- **Reveal on scroll:** `[data-rv]` rises 30px and fades in over .85s with `--ease-out`. There's a 4s failsafe, so nothing stays hidden.
- **Hero image drift:** `[data-drift]`, a gentle parallax.
- **Hover:** color changes and, on the primary button, a 2px lift. Nothing bouncy.
- **Reduced motion:** `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off.

## Content and voice

- **Writing:** plain, warm and specific. Talk like the front desk would ("We'll confirm within the hour.").
- **Real data:** prices, durations and descriptions come from the owner's intake answers (Supabase `site_catalog()`, via `site/js/catalog.js`). Don't hard-code service content.
- **Missing answers:** a missing field shows "Still waiting for details" (`.tbd`, italic hint color).

## Guardrails

- **Shared chrome:** the header, drawer, contact band, map and footer are identical on all five pages. Change one, change all.
- **Width checks:** check 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920+ before shipping. There should be no horizontal scroll, and content should stay aligned to the frame.
- **Accessibility:** visible focus (2px mauve outline), alt text on real photos, and labels on every form field.
