About us

Twenty-two years of helping Tampa glow

Just down the street from Downtown Tampa and UT, we're one of the leading nail salons in the area — specializing in doing everything we can to help you find your glow. Gorgeous nail art or a quiet hour to treat yo' self, we're ready.

Our story

From one small
Davis Islands shop

We opened on Davis Boulevard in 2003 with a handful of chairs and a simple idea: take the time to do it right, and treat everyone who walks in like a regular. Twenty-two years later, a lot of them are.

In 2021 we opened our Kennedy Boulevard location in Hyde Park — same techs, same standard, a little more room to breathe. Some of our clients have been coming for sixteen years and still drive past three other salons to get here.

We're still family-run. We still remember your name, your shape, and the color you almost picked last time.

The standard

What you can count on

Tools that are actually clean

Hospital-grade sterilization, fresh files per guest, and liners in every bowl. Cleanliness isn't a selling point, it's the baseline.

Time to do it right

We don't double-book chairs to shave ten minutes. If your set needs the extra time, it gets the extra time.

Honest pricing

Design and length pricing is on the menu before you sit down. No surprise line items at checkout.

The Citrus Scrub Pedicure

$55 50 minutes Both locations

If you're looking to spoil yourself and not break the bank, this is the one. A citrus soak, a sugar scrub from the toes to the knee, hot towels, and a massage long enough that most people fight to stay awake. Finished with the polish of your choice.

What's included
✦Warm citrus soak and nail shaping
✦Cuticle care and callus smoothing
✦Citrus sugar scrub, toes to knee
✦Hot towel wrap and 15-minute massage
✦Polish, gel, or bare buff finish
Make it yours
Gel finish +$15 Paraffin dip +$8 Extra 10 min massage +$12 French +$10
Book this service Call to book

50 minutes✦Citrus soak✦Sugar scrub✦Hot towels✦Extended massage✦Polish of your choice
How it goes

Fifty minutes, start to finish

01
Soak & settle in

Pick your color from the wall, sink into the chair, and set the massage however you like it. Ten minutes in warm citrus water.

02
Scrub & smooth

Shaping, cuticle care, and callus work, then the citrus sugar scrub from your toes to your knees. Hot towels to finish.

03
Massage & polish

Fifteen minutes of calf and foot massage, then your polish, cured or air-dried. Most people leave slightly sleepy.
